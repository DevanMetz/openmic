OpenMic 0.16.0 adds local playback to the soundboard trim editor.

- Click **Edit** on a pad, adjust **Start** and **End**, then click **Preview** to hear the selection on your headphones or speakers. Preview works with the mic engine stopped and with **Hear soundboard** turned off.
- Click **Stop preview** to end playback early. Changing a trim point or closing the editor also stops the preview. Pads already playing in a call keep their place.

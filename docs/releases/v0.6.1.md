OpenMic 0.6.1 keeps your voice in sync when the computer is under heavy load.

## Fixes

- Your voice no longer falls behind when processing can't keep up, for example while a game is using most of the CPU. OpenMic now skips mic audio that has waited longer than 100 ms instead of playing it back late, and shows a short warning when it does. Before, the delay could reach about 0.7 seconds and stay there.

## For contributors

- `cargo run` without `--release` now compiles OpenMic's own code with light optimization. Unoptimized development builds processed audio slower than real time, which made the voice lag and the window stutter.

## Download and upgrade

OpenMic 0.6.0 offers this update on its own: go to **Settings**, click **Download update**, then **Restart to update**. Or download `openmic-v0.6.1-windows-x64.exe` and run it in place of the previous version.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist). The `.sha256` asset provides the executable's SHA-256 checksum.

OpenMic 0.8.1 gives the strip's space to your soundboard.

## The strip

- The level bar is gone. The microphone itself turns green while your voice goes out, red with a slash when muted, and grey when OpenMic is stopped. Click it to mute or unmute.
- Pad buttons are as wide as their names, so more fit. When the strip gets narrow, the preset goes first and pads stay.
- The strip sizes itself to fit your starred pads and preset. Drag its right edge to set your own width; it keeps that from then on.

## Download and upgrade

Click the version number (or wait for the daily check), then **Update** and **Restart to update**. Or download `openmic-v0.8.1-windows-x64.exe` and run it in place of the previous version.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist). The `.sha256` and `.sig` assets hold the executable's checksum and signature.

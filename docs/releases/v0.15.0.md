OpenMic 0.15.0 makes soundboard clips easier to edit.

- Click **Edit** on a soundboard pad to adjust its volume and trim it using the waveform or start and end times. Trimming does not change the original file.
- Set each clip's volume from 0% to 500%; changes apply to clips already playing. Boosting above 100% may cause distortion. The overall soundboard volume remains separate.

## Download and upgrade

Use the in-app update, or download the Windows executable or Linux archive below. Your settings and soundboard carry over.

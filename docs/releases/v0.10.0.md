OpenMic 0.10.0 keeps recording, dictation and soundboard work responsive, with better keyboard control and recovery when files, devices or background jobs fail.

## Changes

- Drop audio files onto the window to import them into the soundboard. Imports, playback loading, trim waveforms and duration checks run in the background, with progress, cancellation and retry where needed. Finished imports preserve a search query you are actively typing.
- Failed clips stay marked on their pads and appear in red on the strip. Right-click a strip pad, or focus it and press **Shift+F10**, to open its controls on the Soundboard page. Scroll over strip pads to reach those that do not fit.
- Playing and paused clips retain their loaded audio and duration when you edit a trim or source file. A paused clip can resume even if its source becomes unavailable; restarting applies the latest trim and file contents.
- **Tab** and **Shift+Tab** reveal focused controls. Voice settings support arrow keys, **Home** and **End**; soundboard menus support **Shift+F10** and precise typed trim points and percentages. Soundboard names, search text and transcript selections survive background updates, and Dictation focus stays with its control when warnings or recordings change. Dictation controls wrap in a zoomed window, and helper text is easier to read in light and dark themes.
- Recording and microphone tests finish their WAV files in the background. Saving and adding takes to the soundboard also keep microphone controls responsive. Failed captures preserve recoverable audio and warnings; failed saves retain the take for retry. Quitting protects active recordings and unsaved takes.
- **Discard recording** stops an active dictation take. **Cancel transcription** discards the current transcription and queued takes before they type, while retaining the previous transcript. Each take keeps its starting model and shortcut mode, and quitting cancels pending transcription and model downloads.
- Microphone previews recover from cancelled or failed cleanup jobs, retain previous takes when a new test fails, and avoid sending previews to virtual cables or the processed output. Audio processing handles invalid samples, resampling peaks and worker failures more reliably.
- Settings recovery protects unreadable files, update downloads can be cancelled and retried, and startup, shortcut and device errors provide clearer recovery guidance. Restored windows and the strip stay reachable when displays change.

## Download and upgrade

Click the version number (or wait for the daily check), then **Update** and **Restart to update**. Or download `openmic-v0.10.0-windows-x64.exe` and run it in place of the previous version. Your soundboard and settings carry over.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist). The `.sha256` and `.sig` assets hold the executable's checksum and signature.

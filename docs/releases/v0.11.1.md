OpenMic 0.11.1 keeps audio loading and playback going when a file or device reports something unexpected.

## Changes

- A damaged or unusual audio file with no audio channels now fails with a clear error instead of stopping its background loader.
- Audio rate conversion ignores invalid drift values, so an unexpected device timing report cannot stall or interrupt playback.

## Download and upgrade

Click the version number (or wait for the daily check), then **Update** and **Restart to update**. Or download `openmic-v0.11.1-windows-x64.exe` and run it in place of the previous version. Your soundboard and settings carry over.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist). The `.sha256` and `.sig` assets hold the executable's checksum and signature.

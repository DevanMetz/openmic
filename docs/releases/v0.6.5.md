OpenMic 0.6.5 keeps the pinned level meter on top.

## Fixes

- The level meter no longer disappears when you click another window. Windows raises the taskbar (and some other always-on-top windows, like the Snipping Tool) each time a different window comes to the front, which hid a meter placed on or near the taskbar. OpenMic now puts the meter back on top whenever the window in front changes, without taking focus from the app you're using.

## Download and upgrade

Click the version number next to the title (or wait for the daily check), then **Update** and **Restart to update**. Or download `openmic-v0.6.5-windows-x64.exe` and run it in place of the previous version.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist). The `.sha256` and `.sig` assets hold the executable's checksum and signature.

OpenMic 0.6.4 refines the pinned level meter and puts the main voice controls beside the live scope.

## Level meter

- Click the microphone to mute or unmute; a red slash shows when it's muted. The separate Mute button is gone.
- Drag the meter's right edge to resize it, down to just the microphone. When it's narrow, the level runs as a strip under the icon. The size is remembered.
- Fixed the meter looking doubled on setups with monitors at different display scaling. It's now placed once and its position is saved in screen pixels, so it comes back in the same spot on either monitor. After updating, it appears once at the default spot.

## Live scope

- **Your mic**, **To Discord** and **Reduction** are now sliders to the right of the scope, each showing its value. Scroll over a slider for fine steps and double-click it to reset; hovering one highlights the line it controls. You can still drag the blue and green lines on the scope.

## Download and upgrade

Click the version number next to the title (or wait for the daily check), then **Update** and **Restart to update**. Or download `openmic-v0.6.4-windows-x64.exe` and run it in place of the previous version.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist). The `.sha256` and `.sig` assets hold the executable's checksum and signature.

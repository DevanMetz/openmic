OpenMic 0.14.0 adds independent soundboard monitoring.

- Turn on **Hear soundboard** on the Soundboard page to hear clips through the monitor output chosen on the Voice page, without having to turn on **Hear yourself**. The monitor volume applies to both.
- Clip monitoring starts off. Turn it on if you previously heard clips through **Hear yourself** and want to keep hearing them. Clips still go to the processed output for your call.

## Download and upgrade

Use the in-app update, or download the Windows executable or Linux archive below. Your settings and soundboard carry over.

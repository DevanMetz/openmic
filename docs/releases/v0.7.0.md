OpenMic 0.7.0 reorganizes the Microphone tab around what you use most, adds a mic test, and upgrades the soundboard.

## Microphone tab

- **Test my mic**: record 5 seconds, then play it back **Raw** and **Cleaned** to hear exactly what Discord hears. It plays on your headphone monitor (or the Windows default output), never into Discord. **Cleaned** uses your current settings, so you can tune and listen again.
- A **Get set up** checklist for first runs: VB-Cable installed, microphone chosen, Discord's input device, and a mic test. Steps tick off as they're done; hide it any time.
- **Simple** and **Advanced** processing. Simple is a preset plus one **Noise reduction** slider; Advanced shows the model, gates, rumble filter and their charts.
- The level meters now sit beside the **Your mic** and **To Discord** sliders, like a mixer. The Monitor & Levels card is gone: **Headphone monitor** moved to **Routing**, and **Pin meter to screen** to the live scope.

## Soundboard

- Pads show each clip's length and a progress bar while playing.
- Drag a pad onto another to reorder the board. With more than nine clips, a search box filters them.
- Trim a pad from its right-click menu: drag the start and end on the waveform, or type them. The audio file isn't changed.

## Window

- OpenMic reopens at the size and position you left it, on the right monitor.

## Download and upgrade

Click the version number next to the title (or wait for the daily check), then **Update** and **Restart to update**. Or download `openmic-v0.7.0-windows-x64.exe` and run it in place of the previous version. Settings carry over; the setup checklist appears once for existing users, with the steps OpenMic can see already ticked, and can be hidden.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist). The `.sha256` and `.sig` assets hold the executable's checksum and signature.

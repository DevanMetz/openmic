OpenMic 0.9.0 redesigns the Voice page: every setting explains itself and shows what it's doing.

## Voice page

- Each setting is one row, in the order your voice passes through them: **Your mic**, **Remove rumble**, **Remove noise**, **Only my voice**, **Quiet cut-off**, **To Discord** and **Hear yourself**.
- Every row has a plain name, one line saying what it does, a live picture of it working, and its value. The picture is also the control: drag inside it, scroll for fine steps, double-click to reset.
- **Remove noise** shows what comes in (blue shading) against what Discord hears (the green line), so you can see the noise it takes out. Click the model name under it to switch between DeepFilterNet and RNNoise.
- **Only my voice** shades green whenever your voice gets through; drag its line to set how sure it must be that you're talking.
- Switches turn settings on and off, and rows that are off are dimmed.
- Presets are one-click chips above the rows; **+** saves your own.
- The Simple/Advanced switch, the Processing card, the live scope and the mixer strip are replaced by the rows. Nothing appears twice.

## Fixes

- Long device names no longer push the Voice page wider than the window; they're cut short, with the full name on hover.
- The status panel no longer says "Stopped" twice.
- The Soundboard page's hint now points at the Start button in the sidebar, and pads drop the redundant "Play now".

## Download and upgrade

Click the version number (or wait for the daily check), then **Update** and **Restart to update**. Or download `openmic-v0.9.0-windows-x64.exe` and run it in place of the previous version. Your settings carry over unchanged.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist). The `.sha256` and `.sig` assets hold the executable's checksum and signature.

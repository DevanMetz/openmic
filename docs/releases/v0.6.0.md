OpenMic 0.6.0 adds local speech to text, global shortcuts, notification-area controls, recording, and more reliable audio routing.

## New features

- Speech to text with Whisper on your CPU: hold a shortcut to dictate into the focused app, or press once to start and again to stop. Download an English or multilingual model once; transcription stays on your PC.
- Global hotkeys for microphone mute, bypass, stopping clips, dictation, and soundboard pads.
- Notification-area controls, optional startup hidden in the tray, and single-instance handling.
- Record your microphone or computer audio to WAV and save the take or add it directly to the soundboard. Live waveforms show recording progress.
- Per-pad volume, overlapping clips, and saved processing presets.

## Reliability improvements

- Device reconnection, native-format resampling, audio-thread priority, and output clock-drift correction.
- Automatic routing avoids microphone feedback through VB-Cable. Default-microphone changes preserve recovery information and retry failed restoration.
- Atomic settings saves preserve the previous file on failure and show a retry status.
- Recording failures are reported promptly; recoverable partial takes remain available with a warning.
- Dictation text remains available to copy if typing into the target app fails. Held shortcuts release correctly after rebinding, and stalled model downloads time out.
- Fixed clip decoding buffer sizing and Windows reserved recording names.

## Download and upgrade

Download `openmic-v0.6.0-windows-x64.exe`, close the previous OpenMic instance (or choose **Quit OpenMic** from its tray menu, if available), and run the new executable. Existing settings and soundboard entries are migrated automatically.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist). [VB-Audio Virtual Cable](https://vb-audio.com/Cable/) is recommended for routing the cleaned microphone into Discord and other apps. The `.sha256` asset provides the executable's SHA-256 checksum.

Speech models download on request from Hugging Face. Apps running as administrator may reject typed dictation; use Copy in Settings in that case.

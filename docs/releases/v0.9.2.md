OpenMic 0.9.2 makes the strip easier to keep nearby and soundboard clips easier to control.

## Changes

- The strip stays visible when you open the full window. **Settings → General → Always show the strip** controls this behavior and is on by default.
- Removed the **Collapse to strip** button from the main window. You can still minimize to the strip or use the notification-area menu.
- Clicking a playing soundboard pad pauses it. Click again to resume from the same position. Strip buttons work the same way; hotkeys still restart clips.
- Each soundboard pad has a labeled **☆ Add to strip** button. Pinned pads show **★ On strip**; click it to remove them from the strip.
- Hover over a clip's name and click to rename it. **Enter** or clicking elsewhere saves; **Escape** cancels. The saved name appears on the board, in search and on the strip. Leave it blank to restore the filename label. The audio file itself keeps its original name.

## Download and upgrade

Click the version number (or wait for the daily check), then **Update** and **Restart to update**. Or download `openmic-v0.9.2-windows-x64.exe` and run it in place of the previous version. Your soundboard and settings carry over.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist). The `.sha256` and `.sig` assets hold the executable's checksum and signature.

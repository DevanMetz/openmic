OpenMic 0.6.2 makes updates easier to reach and safer to install, fits smaller screens, and keeps a log for troubleshooting.

## New

- Click the version number next to the title to check for updates. Update progress and **Restart to update** now appear at the top of the window instead of on the Settings tab.
- **What's new** shows a release's notes inside OpenMic.
- Signed updates: each release is signed by the release workflow, and OpenMic refuses an update whose signature doesn't match the key built into it. A tampered release can't install itself.
- The window can be resized and starts no taller than your screen; the tabs scroll while the title and the Start/Stop button stay in view. Useful on laptops with display scaling.
- OpenMic keeps a log of device changes, audio warnings, errors and updates. Open it from **Settings → Open log folder** and attach it to bug reports.
- Screen readers can read OpenMic's window.

## For contributors

- Development builds show **dev build** in the title.

## Download and upgrade

From 0.6.0 or 0.6.1, click the version number (or wait for the daily check), then **Update** and **Restart to update**. Or download `openmic-v0.6.2-windows-x64.exe` and run it in place of the previous version.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist). The `.sha256` and `.sig` assets hold the executable's checksum and signature.

OpenMic 0.9.1 puts routing back on the Voice page.

## Changes

- **Routing** (your microphone, the processed output, the monitor output and the default-mic option) is at the top of the **Voice** page again, above the presets and settings. It's no longer under **Settings**.

## Download and upgrade

Click the version number (or wait for the daily check), then **Update** and **Restart to update**. Or download `openmic-v0.9.1-windows-x64.exe` and run it in place of the previous version.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist). The `.sha256` and `.sig` assets hold the executable's checksum and signature.

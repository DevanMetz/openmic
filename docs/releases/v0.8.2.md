OpenMic 0.8.2 makes the minimize button collapse to the strip.

## Changes

- Minimizing the window now collapses OpenMic to the strip, like **Collapse to strip**. Expand the strip (⛶ or double-click) to get the window back.
- Prefer a normal minimize? Untick **Minimize to the strip** under **Settings**.

## Download and upgrade

Click the version number (or wait for the daily check), then **Update** and **Restart to update**. Or download `openmic-v0.8.2-windows-x64.exe` and run it in place of the previous version.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist). The `.sha256` and `.sig` assets hold the executable's checksum and signature.

OpenMic 0.6.3 adds a level meter you can pin to your screen.

## New

- **Pin meter to screen** (under **Monitor & Levels**, or **Pin level meter** in the tray menu) shows a small always-on-top meter of what Discord hears. The mic dot is green while your voice goes out, red when muted, and grey when OpenMic is stopped, next to the output level with a peak marker. It keeps working while OpenMic is hidden in the tray.
- Drag the meter anywhere; it remembers where you put it. Double-click it to open OpenMic, and hover it for **Mute** and **×** to unpin.

## Download and upgrade

Click the version number next to the title (or wait for the daily check), then **Update** and **Restart to update**. Or download `openmic-v0.6.3-windows-x64.exe` and run it in place of the previous version.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and the [Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist). The `.sha256` and `.sig` assets hold the executable's checksum and signature.
